Privacy Policy

This policy explains how {{OPERATOR_NAME}} handles information when an authorized user connects Amazon selling or advertising accounts to the {{OPERATOR_NAME}} Amazon API and Amazon Ads MCP services.

Effective August 6, 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Amazon connection, account-management, and MCP interfaces operated by {{OPERATOR_NAME}}. It covers information received directly from authorized users, from Login with Amazon, and from Amazon APIs after a user grants access.

The services are designed for business and workplace use. They are not directed to children.

2. Data we process

  • Account and workspace identity: employee or administrator identifiers, tenant or workspace identifiers, assigned roles, and connection ownership records.
  • Amazon connection metadata: selling partner identifiers, advertising profile identifiers, account names, marketplaces, regions, currencies, time zones, authorization status, and granted capabilities.
  • Authorization credentials: OAuth authorization codes processed during connection setup, encrypted refresh tokens retained for active connections, and short-lived access tokens used to call Amazon APIs.
  • Requested API data: information returned by Amazon APIs when an authorized user invokes a supported tool. The service minimizes this data and does not use it to build advertising profiles about users.
  • Security and operational records: request identifiers, timestamps, action names, connection status changes, hashed actor references, and error codes used for auditing, reliability, and incident investigation.
  • MCP tool inputs: complete arguments supplied to authenticated tool calls, stored separately in access-restricted diagnostic logs. The HTTP Authorization header and its authentication JWT, as well as complete JSON-RPC requests, are not copied into these files; caller-supplied argument values are retained without redaction.

3. How we use data

We process this information only as needed to:

  • create, maintain, and disconnect Amazon account connections requested by authorized users;
  • verify tenant, employee, grant, and account-binding permissions before each supported action;
  • request and refresh Amazon access credentials and perform authorized Amazon API calls;
  • display connection and advertising profile metadata in the account-management interface;
  • protect the services, investigate failures, maintain audit records, and meet applicable obligations.

{{OPERATOR_NAME}} does not use Amazon data for unrelated advertising, audience building, or independent commercial profiling.

4. Sharing and sale of data

{{OPERATOR_NAME}} does not sell Amazon data. Amazon data is not shared across customers or tenants. Access is limited to the tenant and users whose active grants and bindings authorize the connected account.

Information may be disclosed to Amazon as required to operate the authorized integration, to infrastructure providers acting on our instructions, or when required by law. We do not permit service providers to use Amazon data for their own advertising purposes.

5. Retention, revocation, and deletion

Connection metadata and encrypted refresh tokens are retained only while needed to operate an active connection or satisfy security, dispute, and legal requirements. Operational records are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for auditing and service protection. Restricted MCP tool-input logs are retained for 168 hours and then deleted.

An authorized account owner can disconnect an account from the {{OPERATOR_NAME}} Amazon connection management page. Users may also revoke Login with Amazon access through their Amazon account. Revocation prevents new API access but does not automatically delete records that must be kept for legitimate security or legal purposes.

To request access to or deletion of connection data, email the address below. Include enough information to identify the relevant {{OPERATOR_NAME}} workspace and connection. We may verify the requester's identity and authority before acting on the request.

6. Security

{{OPERATOR_NAME}} uses transport encryption, authenticated access controls, encrypted credential storage, tenant-scoped grants and bindings, least-privilege service boundaries, and audit logging to protect Amazon connection data. OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted at rest and are not exposed in the browser-based account-management interface.

No system is perfectly secure. If we identify an incident that affects covered information, we will investigate and provide notices when required.

7. Service providers and international processing

{{OPERATOR_NAME}} may use hosting, database, security, and monitoring providers to operate the services. These providers process information under contractual or technical restrictions and only for service delivery. Depending on the user's location and the selected Amazon region, information may be processed outside the user's country.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the services, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate service or account channel when required.
